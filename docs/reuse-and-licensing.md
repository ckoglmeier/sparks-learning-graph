## Reusing the database

Keep attribution and license notices with redistributed copies. If you publicly use a derivative database or a work produced from it, ODbL section 4.6 requires an offer of the complete derivative or the prescribed alterations in machine-readable form, free online. This project chooses to supply the complete database. The other ODbL conditions still apply.

The database license does not automatically license unrelated application code. Adapted learning text and other borrowed assets have their own obligations. Avoid imposing conflicting restrictions on licensed data/content through application terms or technical controls.

## Publication approach and remaining limits

This public edition retains all curriculum identifiers and topic links while omitting every standard-text payload from `data/curriculum-standards.json`. England, ACARA and Common Core source notices are retained for provenance, even though their catalogue text is no longer distributed here.

The inherited teaching-research layer is preserved. Upstream removed some matching curriculum quotations, but that mechanical process is not a comprehensive rights audit. Short quotations, paraphrases and Common Core cross-references may remain subject to their original terms. This is not a claim that every byte has one unrestricted license.

References to Next Generation Science Standards* identify the source framework. No logos, endorsement badges or certification claims are used. WestEd’s [commercial trademark guidance](https://www.nextgenscience.org/ngss-trademarks-and-copyright/ngss-trademarks-and-copyright) requests proposed-use review. No special approval has been obtained and no permission request has been sent. The maintainer has chosen to proceed with this limited reference use and explicit disclosures; publication is not evidence of publisher approval or legal clearance. Reusers must assess their own use.

Report specific attribution or rights concerns through the repository’s issues, identifying the file and passage so it can be reviewed and corrected. Do not post personal or confidential information in a public issue.
