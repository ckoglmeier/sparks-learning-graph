# Curriculum relationships and enrichment

A useful engineering concept need not map one-to-one to a curriculum expectation. This release makes that distinction visible instead of stretching a standards link to fit.

**Scope:** selected England 2013 Science, Computing and Design & Technology programmes, plus the Next Generation Science Standards* 5-PS1-3 assessment boundary. This is not a worldwide curriculum audit, and no newly authored topic is advertised as fully curriculum-aligned. Existing upstream associations are preserved, not recertified.

All ten new concepts currently have proposed global age contexts and no formal standard associations. That does **not** mean that all ten are absent from school curricula. Some contribute to primary requirements, some introduce later-stage ideas qualitatively, and some are applications for which an exact mapping has not been established.

| Concept | Relationship | Coverage disposition and boundary |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Comparing density using equal volumes | enrichment with explicit assessment exclusion | **Out of scope** — The proposed density comparison is outside this particular performance expectation's assessment boundary. Do not map density mastery to 5-PS1-3. This is not a claim that all elementary curricula exclude density. |
| Water displaced by immersed objects | enrichment in selected primary scope | **Unverified** — Displacement is a useful conceptual prerequisite/application for buoyancy; no exact primary expectation was established in this review. Do not infer full coverage of fluid pressure from displaced-volume comparisons; other jurisdictions remain unreviewed. |
| Buoyant support and floating | earlier qualitative introduction | **Partial** — Related to the later-stage floating/upthrust requirement; the primary-age claim supplies a bounded qualitative model. Does not cover the complete pressure strand, atmosphere or numerical pressure; proposed ages are not statutory age mappings. |
| Pressure in still liquids | earlier qualitative introduction | **Partial** — Direct conceptual overlap with depth-dependent liquid pressure; the proposed younger scope omits numerical requirements. The complete strand also includes atmospheric and force/area pressure. No primary statutory association is authored. |
| Sensing and acting in physical control systems | component of primary requirements | **Partial** — Explaining sensing/control/actuation supports physical-system programming and product control. The conceptual explanation alone does not demonstrate writing/debugging a program, decomposition or designing/making a product; see the D&T source as well. |
| Correcting a system using feedback | component with enrichment | **Partial** — Repeated measurement and correction can support programming, monitoring and control. This regulator model is an authored application, not an explicit primary requirement for closed-loop theory; explanation alone does not establish programming or making competence. |
| Force and travel in simple machines | extends related primary content | **Partial** — Related primary content covers mechanisms that change force; the explicit input-travel and energy relationship adds depth. No numerical work or efficiency coverage; a concept task alone does not meet practical product-use conditions. Related examples do not certify every mechanism expectation. |
| Load placement and static tipping | component with enrichment | **Partial** — Qualitative stability prediction contributes to understanding structures; the explicit centre/support model extends the primary practical requirement. Does not cover actually building stronger/stiffer/stable structures; formal turning-effects content is later-stage. No KS1 age mapping is inferred for this 8–12 proposal. |
| Contact force spread over an area | earlier qualitative introduction | **Partial** — The direction-of-change comparison addresses a qualitative component of later force/area pressure. Does not assess the numerical ratio or the whole pressure strand; primary-age use is proposed enrichment. |
| Solar-panel orientation and light capture | application enrichment | **Unverified** — Related light, electricity and design content provides foundations; no exact primary panel-orientation requirement was established. Do not label a working circuit or generic energy-conversion standard fully covered by this orientation concept. Other jurisdictions were not checked. |

## Read the labels carefully

“Partial” is an authoring judgment about coverage of the named requirement, not a learner score. A conceptual explanation may contribute while leaving required investigation, programming, construction or quantitative work unassessed. “Unverified” means no exact association has been established. “Out of scope” is used for the named NGSS assessment exclusion, not for the entire subject.

“Enrichment” describes the relationship of our bounded claim to the selected curriculum scope. It does not imply that a curriculum is deficient, that a topic is unsuitable for children, or that an earlier teaching age has been validated. Later-stage placement can explain why a proposed concrete introduction has no exact primary mapping.

## Sources and inspected sections

- [England Science](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-science-programmes-of-study): Year 5 Forces; KS3 Motion and forces, including turning effects and pressure. Primary light/electricity sections were also compared for the solar application.
- [England Design & Technology](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-design-and-technology-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-design-and-technology-programmes-of-study): KS1 and KS2 technical knowledge. Practical design/make conditions are retained as conditions rather than inferred from conceptual explanations.
- [England Computing](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/national-curriculum-in-england-computing-programmes-of-study/national-curriculum-in-england-computing-programmes-of-study): KS1 and KS2 content, especially physical-system programs, decomposition, sequence, selection, repetition, variables and inputs/outputs.
- [NGSS 5-PS1-3](https://www.nextgenscience.org/pe/5-ps1-3-matter-and-its-interactions): the explicit assessment boundary. This release links the source and factual code; it does not reproduce restricted source wording.

Sources read 24 November 2026. Machine-readable dispositions are in [curriculum-alignment.json](curriculum-alignment.json). These reports intentionally remain outside the strict upstream alignment schema. Formal associations should be added only after a scoped mapping pass establishes exact source identity, age context, contribution and remaining conditions.

The public story supported by this release is that practical engineering prompted us to make some implicit relationships explicit—and to document where those relationships extend beyond a particular primary requirement. It does not support a claim that games outperform school curricula or that these activities produce proven learning gains.
